# Allotment plot 14

## Layout

Eight beds, 1.2 m wide, no-dig, mulched with 5 cm of compost each autumn.

## Routine

The sowing calendar check, on the first of each month.

## Problems

Slugs on brassicas: beer traps and a torch walk after rain.
