# Bakery wholesale — supplying three cafés

## Current state

- Three cafés (Mill Lane, the Corner Room, Ferry Café) take a daily order.
- The van leaves at 6:30.
- Orders close at 2pm the day before.
- Prices: cost times three, reviewed each quarter.

## Goal

A fourth café by spring without adding a second early shift.

## Update (2026-09-02)

The order cut-off moved from 2pm to noon the day before, so the evening shift
can plan the mix before it starts.
