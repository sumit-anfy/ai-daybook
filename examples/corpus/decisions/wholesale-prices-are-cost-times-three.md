# Wholesale prices are cost times three

## The rule

Price = (ingredient cost + labour minutes × hourly wage ÷ 60) × 3, rounded up to
the nearest 5p.

## Review

Once a quarter, from the supplier price sheet. Not on every flour price change.

## Alternatives

Matching the bakery across town. Rejected: their rent and wages are not ours.
