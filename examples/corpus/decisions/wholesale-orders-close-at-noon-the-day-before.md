# Wholesale orders close at noon the day before

## Why

At 2pm the evening shift had already started weighing when the last orders
came in, and twice in August the rye was short. Noon gives them the full mix.

## Late orders

A late order goes on the next day's van. No exceptions, so no one has to judge
which café deserves one.
