# Baker's percentages

## Definition

Every ingredient is written as a percentage of the total flour weight. Flour is
100%. Water at 70% means 700 g of water for every 1 kg of flour.

## Typical values

| Bread | Hydration |
|---|---|
| Sandwich loaf | 60–65% |
| Sourdough | 70–80% |
| Rye | 80–90% |
