# Playbook — weekly wholesale order intake

## Steps

1. At noon (the cut-off), open the order inbox.
2. Reply to each café confirming its order.
3. Add each order to the bake sheet by product line.
4. Total the flour by type and check it against stock.
5. Hand the sheet to the evening shift.

## Late orders

Anything after noon goes on the next day's van. Reply saying so.
