# Playbook — session close-out

## The four parts

1. **Keep** — anything now true. Each ruling gets its own decision or pattern
   node; the session cites the id.
2. **Conflicts** — which existing node this makes false. That node gets a dated
   correction, or is superseded.
3. **Your call** — what needs a human decision.
4. **Tasks** — what to do next, one line each. They go to the task app.

## Why

Most sessions produce nothing durable, and saying "nothing to keep" is the right
answer. But it has to be said: silence and forgetting look the same.
