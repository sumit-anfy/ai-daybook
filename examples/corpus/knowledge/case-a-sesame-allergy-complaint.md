# Case — a sesame allergy complaint

## What happened

Mr Haddad reacted to a plain sourdough. Seeded loaves had cooled on the same
rack and shed seeds onto it.

## What changed

Seeded loaves cool on their own labelled rack, on the bottom shelf.
