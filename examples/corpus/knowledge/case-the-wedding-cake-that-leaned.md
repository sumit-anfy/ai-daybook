# Case — the wedding cake that leaned

## What happened

The Okafor wedding cake leaned on arrival. The dowels were cut to the lower tier
only, and the courier braked hard.

## What changed

Every tiered cake is now dowelled through the whole stack and goes in the shop
van, not by courier.
